# Detect Overcommitment

Identify when a plan or schedule commits more than available capacity.

## When to use

- Detect overcommitment in tasks and plans. Use when asked to check if a schedule is overloaded, find overcommitment, or review capacity.

## Workflow

1. Gather tasks with time estimates and the available capacity window.
2. Sum the estimated effort and compare with capacity.
3. Flag overload periods and stacked deadlines.
4. Check for dependencies that compound risk.
5. Suggest what to defer, drop, or re-scope.

## Output format

An overcommitment analysis: capacity vs. load, risk periods, and recommendations.

## Constraints

- Use provided estimates honestly; flag if estimates are missing.
- Do not silently compress work to make it fit.
