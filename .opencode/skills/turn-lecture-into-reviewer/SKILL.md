# Turn Lecture into Reviewer

Transform lecture content into a structured reviewer for efficient study.

## When to use

- Convert lecture content into a structured reviewer (study material). Use when asked to turn lecture notes into a reviewer, study guide, or revision material.

## Workflow

1. Read the lecture content and identify the learning objectives.
2. Structure: key concepts, definitions, formulas, examples, common pitfalls.
3. Convert content into digestible study chunks.
4. Add self-test questions or recall prompts.
5. Keep it organized for fast revision.

## Output format

A structured reviewer: concepts, definitions, examples, self-test questions.

## Constraints

- Stay within the lecture's content scope.
- Prioritize exam-relevant and core material.
