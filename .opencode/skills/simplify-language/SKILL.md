# Simplify Language

Reduce reading difficulty by simplifying vocabulary and sentence structure.

## When to use

- Simplify text into clearer and easier language. Use when asked to simplify language, make text easier to read, or lower reading difficulty.

## Workflow

1. Identify complex vocabulary, long sentences, and passive constructions.
2. Replace hard words with common alternatives.
3. Break long sentences into shorter ones.
4. Prefer active voice and concrete language.
5. Verify meaning and key details are fully preserved.

## Output format

Simplified text that is easier to read with the same meaning.

## Constraints

- Preserve technical accuracy - do not distort specialized content.
- Keep important nuance and numbers intact.
