# PRD from Raw Notes

Transform unstructured product notes into a clean, complete Product Requirements Document.

## When to use

- Turn messy product notes into a structured PRD. Use when asked to create a PRD, product requirements document, or structure raw product brainstorming notes.

## Workflow

1. Collect all raw notes and group them by theme: problem, users, solution, requirements, scope.
2. Define the problem statement and the user(s) affected.
3. Derive goals and success metrics from the notes.
4. List functional requirements, prioritized (must/should/could).
5. Capture constraints, assumptions, and open questions explicitly.
6. Write the PRD in a standard structure; flag gaps where notes were insufficient.

## Output format

Structured PRD: Title, Context/Problem, Goals & Metrics, Users, Functional Requirements (MoSCoW), Non-functional requirements, Constraints, Open Questions.

## Constraints

- Only use information present in the notes; mark inferred content clearly.
- Keep requirements testable and unambiguous.
