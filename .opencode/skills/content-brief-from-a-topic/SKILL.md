# Content Brief from a Topic

Convert a bare topic into a complete content brief: target audience, angle, key points, outline, and writing guidance.

## When to use

- Turn a topic into a ready-to-write content brief with an outline and key points. Use when asked to create a content brief, outline an article, plan a blog post, or structure a piece before writing.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the topic and ask for audience, goal, and desired length if not provided.
2. Define the angle: the specific take or promise the content makes to the reader.
3. Identify the target audience and what they need to know or do after reading.
4. Brainstorm 3-7 key points that support the angle, ordered for narrative flow.
5. Build a section-by-section outline with working headings and 1-2 bullet notes each.
6. Add practical guidance: tone, voice, length, SEO keywords (if relevant), and calls to action.

## Output format

A structured content brief with: Topic, Goal, Audience, Angle, Key points, Outline (headings + bullets), Tone guidance.

## Constraints

- Keep the brief concise enough to scan in under a minute.
- Do not write the full article unless asked - the brief is the deliverable.
