# Break Idea into Business Model

Decompose a raw business idea into a structured, testable business model.

## When to use

- Structure a business idea into a clear and actionable business model. Use when asked to break an idea into a business model, validate a business concept, or map out how an idea makes money.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the idea and the customer problem it solves.
2. Define: customer segments, value proposition, channels.
3. Map revenue streams and cost structure.
4. Identify key resources, activities, and partners.
5. Define key metrics to validate the model.
6. Present as a lean canvas / business model summary.

## Output format

A structured business model (lean-canvas style) with validation metrics.

## Constraints

- Ground assumptions explicitly - label them as hypotheses.
- Do not invent market numbers; use placeholders.
