# Polish Wording

Improve word choice and phrasing so text sounds natural and precise.

## When to use

- Refine wording for clearer and more natural phrasing. Use when asked to polish wording, refine phrasing, or make text sound more natural.

## Workflow

1. Read the text and flag awkward, clunky, or imprecise phrasing.
2. Replace each flagged phrase with clearer, more natural wording.
3. Sharpen word choices: stronger verbs, precise nouns.
4. Keep the author's voice and meaning intact.
5. Check the result reads smoothly aloud.

## Output format

The text with refined, more natural wording.

## Constraints

- Light-touch editing - do not restructure the whole piece.
- Preserve meaning, tone, and the author's distinctive voice.
