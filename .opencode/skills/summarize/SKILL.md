# Summarize

Produce a clear, accurate summary of the key points of any content, scaled to the user's requested depth and adapted to the content type.

## When to use

- Turn any content - long text, pages, documents, or notes - into a clear, concise summary at the requested depth, including key ideas, decisions, action items, and conclusions. Use when asked to summarize, create a summary, get key points or a TL;DR, or condense meeting/class notes.

## Workflow

1. Read the full content and identify its type (article, page, meeting notes, email, spec...).
2. Identify the main topic, argument, and purpose.
3. Extract the key points, arguments, and conclusions, keeping their relative weight.
4. For notes or action-oriented content: remove duplication and shorthand, then extract decisions, action items (with owners/dates if present), and open questions.
5. Write the summary at the requested depth: TL;DR (1-2 lines), short bullets, or structured sections.
6. Verify every point and claim traces back to the source.

## Output format

A concise summary at the requested level of detail - TL;DR, bullets, or structured sections (Key Ideas / Decisions / Action Items / Conclusions).

## Constraints

- Stay faithful to the source - no new claims; never invent action items, owners, or facts.
- Keep the summary much shorter than the original.
- Distinguish facts from interpretation.
