# Summarize

Produce a clear, concise summary capturing the key points of long content.

## When to use

- Turn long content into a clear, concise summary of the key points. Use when asked to summarize, create a summary, or get the key points of long content.

## Workflow

1. Read the full content to capture the complete picture.
2. Identify the main argument or topic.
3. Extract the key points, keeping their relative weight.
4. Write a summary at the requested depth (TL;DR, bullets, or structured).
5. Verify every point traces back to the source.

## Output format

A concise summary of the key points at the requested level of detail.

## Constraints

- Stay faithful to the source - no new claims.
- Keep the summary much shorter than the original.
