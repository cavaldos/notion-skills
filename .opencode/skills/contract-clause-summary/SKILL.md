# Contract Clause Summary

Explain a legal clause in plain language while flagging risk areas.

## When to use

- Translate a dense legal clause into plain language, with guardrails. Use when asked to explain a contract clause, summarize legal text, or translate legalese.

## Workflow

1. Read the clause carefully and identify its operative parts.
2. Translate each part into plain language, keeping the legal meaning.
3. Note what obligations, rights, and deadlines each party has.
4. Flag risk areas: one-sided terms, ambiguity, unusual obligations.
5. Explicitly state this is not legal advice and suggest review by counsel.

## Output format

A plain-language clause summary with a risk-flag section.

## Constraints

- Do not give legal conclusions or predictions of enforceability.
- Mark anything uncertain rather than guessing.
