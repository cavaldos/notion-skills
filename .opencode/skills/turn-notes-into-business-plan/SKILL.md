# Turn Notes into Business Plan

Turn scattered business notes into a structured, coherent business plan.

## When to use

- Convert raw notes into a structured business plan. Use when asked to write a business plan from notes or structure business planning notes.

## Workflow

1. Review the notes and identify the business's core idea.
2. Structure: Executive summary, Problem, Solution, Market, Business model, Go-to-market, Team, Financials, Risks.
3. Fill each section from the notes; expand into clear prose.
4. Mark gaps where notes lack information (financials especially).
5. Keep the plan honest and investor-readable.

## Output format

A structured business plan built from the notes, with flagged gaps.

## Constraints

- Do not invent market size or financials - use placeholders.
- Stay faithful to the founder's notes and vision.
