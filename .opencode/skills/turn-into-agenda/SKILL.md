# Turn into Agenda

Convert raw topics into a structured, time-boxed meeting agenda.

## When to use

- Turn raw topics or notes into a structured, time-boxed meeting agenda. Use when asked to create a meeting agenda, structure a meeting, or plan a session.

## Workflow

1. Collect the raw topics and clarify the meeting's goal.
2. Order topics by importance and dependency.
3. Estimate and assign time boxes to each topic.
4. Add: owner/facilitator per topic, desired outcome, and prep notes.
5. Include buffer time and a decision/action close.

## Output format

A time-boxed agenda: goal, topics with owners/outcomes/time, prep notes.

## Constraints

- Total time should match the meeting length; flag overrun.
- Keep each topic outcome-specific, not vague.
