# Highlight Important Concepts

Identify and emphasize the most important concepts in any content.

## When to use

- Automatically identify and highlight the most important concepts from content. Use when asked to highlight key concepts, mark important ideas, or extract what matters most from a text.

## Workflow

1. Read the content to understand its subject and purpose.
2. Identify the concepts that carry the most weight (repeated, central, actionable).
3. Rank them by importance to the reader's goal.
4. Present each with a short explanation of why it matters.
5. Format clearly: bolded concept + 1-2 line note, or a list.

## Output format

A prioritized list of important concepts with brief explanations.

## Constraints

- Only concepts actually in the content - no external additions.
- Keep explanations tight.
