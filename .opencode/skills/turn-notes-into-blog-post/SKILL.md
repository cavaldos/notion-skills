# Turn Notes into Blog Post

Shape raw notes into a publishable blog post with clear structure and flow.

## When to use

- Convert notes into a structured, readable blog post. Use when asked to turn notes into a blog post or draft a blog article from notes.

## Workflow

1. Read the notes and identify the post's core thesis.
2. Define the audience and the promised takeaway.
3. Structure: title, hook, body sections, conclusion, optional CTA.
4. Write the post from the notes, expanding ideas into full prose.
5. Add transitions and a compelling opening and closing.
6. Keep the author's voice and only use note content.

## Output format

A complete blog post draft (title + body) derived from the notes.

## Constraints

- Stay within the notes' content; flag anything needing research.
- Do not pad with generic filler to reach a length.
