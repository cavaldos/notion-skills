# Rewrite Tone (Formal/Casual)

Shift a piece of text between formal and casual registers while keeping meaning.

## When to use

- Rewrite text in a formal or casual tone. Use when asked to make text more formal, more casual, or change its tone.

## Workflow

1. Read the text and confirm the target tone (formal or casual).
2. Adjust vocabulary: formal uses precise/latinate terms; casual uses everyday words.
3. Adjust sentence structure: formal favors complete, complex sentences; casual favors short, conversational ones.
4. Adjust contractions, pronouns, and politeness markers accordingly.
5. Keep all facts and meaning intact.

## Output format

The same content rewritten in the requested tone.

## Constraints

- Match the extreme requested - do not half-shift the tone.
- Preserve meaning, facts, and structure.
