# Turn Content into Carousel

Split any content into a slide-by-slide carousel with clear structure.

## When to use

- Convert content into a structured carousel format. Use when asked to turn content into a carousel, slide deck, or multi-slide social post.

## Workflow

1. Read the content and identify the narrative arc.
2. Define the slide sequence: hook slide, key points, examples, CTA.
3. Write each slide's headline and supporting text (short!).
4. Ensure each slide stands alone while building on the last.
5. Add design hints: suggested visuals, layout, and emphasis.

## Output format

A carousel plan: slide-by-slide headlines, copy, and design hints.

## Constraints

- Slides must be short - one idea per slide.
- Stay faithful to the source content.
