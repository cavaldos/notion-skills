# Identify Bottlenecks

Locate the points in a process where work stalls, queues build, or throughput drops.

## When to use

- Identify bottlenecks in processes or workflows. Use when asked to find bottlenecks, diagnose slow processes, or spot where work gets stuck.

## Workflow

1. Map the process steps in order with their inputs and outputs.
2. Identify steps with queues, waiting, rework, or high failure rates.
3. Find the step that constrains overall throughput (the slowest link).
4. Assess why each bottleneck exists: capacity, dependency, quality, handoffs.
5. Recommend targeted fixes and the expected impact of each.

## Output format

A bottleneck analysis: process map, bottleneck locations, causes, and prioritized fixes.

## Constraints

- Base conclusions on the provided process information - do not invent metrics.
- Focus on the highest-leverage bottleneck, not every minor inefficiency.
