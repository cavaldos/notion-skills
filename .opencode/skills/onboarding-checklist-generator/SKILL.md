# Onboarding Checklist Generator

Produce a complete onboarding checklist tailored to a specific role or team.

## When to use

- Generate a tailored onboarding checklist for a role or team. Use when asked to create an onboarding checklist, onboarding plan, or new-hire checklist.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the role, team, and available onboarding resources.
2. Structure by week or stage: pre-start, week 1, week 2, first month, first quarter.
3. Cover: access/setup, people introductions, training, first tasks, goals.
4. Make each item concrete and checkable.
5. Include owners and expected completion times.

## Output format

A stage-based onboarding checklist with owners and timing.

## Constraints

- Tailor to the role - no generic filler.
- Keep items realistic for the given timeline.
