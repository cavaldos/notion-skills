# Rewrite for Twitter/X

Adapt content into concise, punchy X/Twitter posts or thread format.

## When to use

- Rewrite content to fit Twitter/X's concise and structured format. Use when asked to adapt content for Twitter/X posts or threads.

## Workflow

1. Extract the core message from the source content.
2. Compress into a tight post: hook line, key point, takeaway.
3. For threads: split into numbered posts that flow logically.
4. Use line breaks and short paragraphs for readability.
5. Match the platform's informal, direct energy without losing substance.

## Output format

X/Twitter-ready post or numbered thread.

## Constraints

- Respect the message - compression, not distortion.
- Keep posts scannable; no giant blocks of text.
