# Policy Q&A Formatter

Convert dense policy text into a clean, scannable Q&A format.

## When to use

- Turn raw policy text into a clean, scannable FAQ. Use when asked to format policy text as FAQ, create a Q&A from policy, or make policy easier to scan.

## Workflow

1. Read the policy and identify the topics users will ask about.
2. Draft a question for each topic in plain user language.
3. Answer each question concisely from the policy text.
4. Keep the policy's meaning and requirements intact.
5. Order questions by importance and frequency.

## Output format

A scannable FAQ derived strictly from the policy text.

## Constraints

- Answers must not soften or alter policy requirements.
- Quote or paraphrase faithfully - no interpretation drift.
