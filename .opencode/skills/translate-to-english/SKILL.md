# Translate to English

Translate content into natural, fluent English, keeping structure and formatting intact.

## When to use

- Translate content from any language into clear, natural English while preserving formatting. Use when asked to translate to English.

## Workflow

1. Read the full source to capture meaning, tone, and register.
2. Translate into natural English - not word-for-word but meaning-for-meaning.
3. Preserve formatting: headings, lists, tables, emphasis.
4. Keep proper nouns, brands, and standard terms untranslated.
5. Adjust idioms and cultural references to English equivalents.
6. Re-read for flow and verify accuracy against the source.

## Output format

Full English translation with original formatting preserved.

## Constraints

- Do not paraphrase away important details or numbers.
- Match the source's tone: formal stays formal, casual stays casual.
