# Meeting Prep Brief

Produce a pre-meeting brief: topic context, attendee context, goals, and questions.

## When to use

- Walk into a meeting prepared with context on the people and the topic. Use when asked to prepare for a meeting, create a meeting prep brief, or research attendees.

## Workflow

1. Gather the meeting topic, agenda, and your goals.
2. Summarize relevant context: recent status, decisions, open items.
3. Profile key attendees: role, interests, stance if known.
4. List the questions to ask and points to make.
5. Define desired outcomes and follow-up plan.

## Output format

A meeting prep brief: context, attendees, goals, questions, outcomes.

## Constraints

- Only include known information about attendees - no assumptions.
- Keep the brief scannable in the minutes before the meeting.
