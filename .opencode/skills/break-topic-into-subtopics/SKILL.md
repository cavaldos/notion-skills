# Break Topic into Subtopics

Divide a broad topic into a clear, structured set of subtopics.

## When to use

- Divide a topic into clear and structured subtopics. Use when asked to break a topic into subtopics, outline a subject, or structure a knowledge area.

## Workflow

1. Define the topic and its boundaries.
2. Identify the natural dimensions of the topic.
3. List subtopics under each dimension.
4. Check they are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive enough.
5. Present as an outline with depth levels.

## Output format

A structured outline of subtopics with hierarchy.

## Constraints

- Subtopics must stay within the topic's scope.
- Keep granularity proportional to the topic's size.
