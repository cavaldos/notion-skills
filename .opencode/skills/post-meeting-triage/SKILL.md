# Post-Meeting Triage

In one pass: produce a meeting summary, extract tasks, and prepare a log update.

## When to use

- Summary, tasks, and log update from a meeting in a single pass. Use when asked to triage meeting notes, process a meeting afterward, or update logs from a meeting.

## Workflow

1. Read the meeting notes and extract the outcome.
2. Write a tight summary: what happened and why it matters.
3. Extract tasks with owners and dates.
4. Prepare a log-update snippet (decision log / status log).
5. Deliver all three in a structured bundle.

## Output format

A triage bundle: Summary, Action items, Decision-log entry.

## Constraints

- Single source of truth: only the meeting notes.
- Keep the log entry format consistent with existing logs.
