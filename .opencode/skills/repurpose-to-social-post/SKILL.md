# Repurpose to Social Post

Extract the essence of a document and reshape it into a scroll-stopping social post.

## When to use

- Turn a doc or notes into a ready-to-post social update. Use when asked to convert content into a social post, LinkedIn post, tweet thread, or social update.

## Workflow

1. Read the source and identify the single most valuable idea or insight.
2. Write a hook that earns attention in the first line.
3. Structure the post: hook, insight, supporting detail, takeaway, call to action.
4. Adapt length and format to the target platform (LinkedIn, X, Threads, etc.).
5. Add platform-appropriate formatting: line breaks, emojis (sparingly), hashtags if requested.
6. Proofread and verify the post is faithful to the source.

## Output format

A ready-to-paste social post, with platform noted.

## Constraints

- Do not distort the source's meaning for engagement.
- No emojis/hashtags unless the user asks or the platform convention demands them.
