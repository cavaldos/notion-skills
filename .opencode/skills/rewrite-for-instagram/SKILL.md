# Rewrite for Instagram

Adapt any content into Instagram-native copy: short, visual, scannable, engaging.

## When to use

- Rewrite content to fit Instagram's style and format. Use when asked to adapt content for Instagram captions, carousels, or Reels.

## Workflow

1. Extract the core message and emotional hook from the source.
2. Compress into a caption: 1-3 short sentences plus optional CTA.
3. Use line breaks and light formatting for mobile readability.
4. For carousels: split content into slide-by-slide chunks with clear headers.
5. Include a call to action (save, share, comment, link) if appropriate.
6. Keep the brand voice; Instagram-style does not mean generic.

## Output format

Instagram-ready copy: caption and/or carousel slide plan.

## Constraints

- Respect the platform: no long paragraphs, no walls of text.
- Do not add hashtag spam unless explicitly requested.
