# Discovery Call Recap

Convert discovery call notes into a tidy summary with clear follow-ups.

## When to use

- Turn a discovery call into a tidy summary and clear follow-ups. Use when asked to recap a discovery call, summarize a sales call, or list call follow-ups.

## Workflow

1. Extract the customer's situation, needs, and pain points.
2. Summarize what was discussed and any product-fit signals.
3. List agreed next steps with owner and timeline.
4. Note open questions and objections to address.
5. Structure: Context, Key takeaways, Next steps, Open questions.

## Output format

A discovery call recap with structured follow-ups.

## Constraints

- Only include what was actually discussed.
- Be specific about follow-up owners and dates.
