# Support Reply Drafter

Turn a customer ticket into a professional, on-brand support reply.

## When to use

- Draft an on-brand support reply from a customer ticket. Use when asked to draft a customer support response, reply to a ticket, or write a support email.

## Workflow

1. Read the ticket and identify the issue, tone, and urgency.
2. Summarize the issue back to confirm understanding.
3. Provide the resolution or next steps clearly, in order.
4. Match the brand's voice and empathy level (professional, friendly, apologetic as needed).
5. Keep it concise; add an offer to help further.
6. Flag if the ticket lacks information needed to resolve it.

## Output format

A ready-to-send support reply.

## Constraints

- Do not promise timelines, refunds, or actions you cannot verify.
- Never blame the customer, even implicitly.
