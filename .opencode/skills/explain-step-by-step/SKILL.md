# Explain Step-by-Step

Explain a concept or process as clear, ordered steps.

## When to use

- Break down a concept or process into clear step-by-step explanations. Use when asked to explain step by step, walk through a process, or explain how something works sequentially.

## Workflow

1. Identify the concept or process to explain.
2. Break it into its logical sequential steps.
3. Explain each step in plain language with the 'why'.
4. Add an example walkthrough if helpful.
5. Summarize the sequence at the end.

## Output format

A numbered step-by-step explanation with example.

## Constraints

- Steps must be genuinely sequential and complete.
- Keep each step explanation clear and brief.
