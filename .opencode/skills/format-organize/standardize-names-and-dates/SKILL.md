# Standardize Names & Dates

Normalize inconsistent names, terminology, and date formats across a document or dataset.

## When to use

- Apply consistent casing, terminology, and date formatting across content. Use when asked to standardize formatting, normalize names/dates, or enforce consistent conventions.

## Workflow

1. Identify the variants: casing differences, term synonyms, date formats.
2. Determine the canonical form for each (most common or user-specified).
3. Apply the canonical form consistently everywhere.
4. Keep a mapping of what was changed for transparency.
5. Verify no two variants of the same thing remain.

## Output format

Standardized content plus a change map (old form → new form).

## Constraints

- Do not change meaning - only representation.
- Ask before choosing a canonical form if none is dominant.
