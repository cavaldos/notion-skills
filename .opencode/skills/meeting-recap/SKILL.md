# Meeting Recap

Convert meeting notes into a clear recap: what happened, what was decided, what happens next.

## When to use

- Turn a meeting into a clear recap with decisions and next steps. Use when asked to recap a meeting, write meeting notes, or summarize what was decided.

## Workflow

1. Extract the meeting's purpose and outcome.
2. List the key decisions made (with who and why where stated).
3. Extract action items with owner and due date where available.
4. Note open questions and unresolved topics.
5. Structure: Summary, Decisions, Action Items, Open Questions.

## Output format

A structured meeting recap with decisions and next steps.

## Constraints

- Do not invent decisions or action items not in the notes.
- Keep it concise - a recap is not a transcript.
