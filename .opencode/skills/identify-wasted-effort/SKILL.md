# Identify Wasted Effort

Scan a task, process, or workflow and surface places where effort is spent without proportional value.

## When to use

- Identify wasted effort in tasks or workflows. Use when asked to audit a process, find inefficiencies, spot redundant work, or review where time/energy is being lost.

## Workflow

1. Map the current workflow or task list end to end.
2. For each step, classify its output: value-producing, supporting, or busywork.
3. Flag steps that are duplicated, low-impact, or done out of order.
4. Estimate the effort each flagged step consumes (time, energy, cost).
5. Recommend what to stop, automate, delegate, or batch.
6. Prioritize recommendations by effort saved vs. risk/change cost.

## Output format

A wasted-effort audit: workflow map, flagged steps with reasoning, and prioritized recommendations (stop / automate / delegate / batch).

## Constraints

- Be specific - point at concrete steps, not general vibes.
- Only recommend changes that are actionable within the user's context.
