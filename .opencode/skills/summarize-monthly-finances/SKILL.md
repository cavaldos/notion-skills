# Summarize Monthly Finances

Produce a clear monthly financial summary from transactions or records.

## When to use

- Summarize monthly finances. Use when asked to summarize monthly finances, review a month of spending, or create a monthly financial summary.

## Workflow

1. Review the month's transactions/records.
2. Compute totals: income, expenses, savings, by category.
3. Compare against budget or previous month if available.
4. Spot anomalies, big changes, and notable patterns.
5. Present a clear summary with the key takeaways first.

## Output format

A monthly finance summary: totals, category breakdown, insights.

## Constraints

- Use only the provided financial data.
- Distinguish facts from interpretation.
