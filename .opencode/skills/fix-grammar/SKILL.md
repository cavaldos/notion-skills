# Fix Grammar

Correct grammar errors and improve sentence clarity with minimal disruption.

## When to use

- Correct grammar and sentence clarity. Use when asked to fix grammar, correct a text, or clean up sentence errors.

## Workflow

1. Read the text to understand meaning before editing.
2. Fix grammar, punctuation, and spelling errors.
3. Repair ambiguous or broken sentences for clarity.
4. Keep the author's voice and original structure.
5. Show corrections (inline or diff-style) if requested.

## Output format

The corrected text with grammar and clarity fixes.

## Constraints

- Do not change meaning or restyle the text.
- Only touch sentences that are genuinely broken or unclear.
