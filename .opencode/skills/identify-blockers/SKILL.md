# Identify Blockers

Locate what is preventing tasks or workflows from progressing.

## When to use

- Identify blockers in tasks or workflows. Use when asked to find blockers, identify what's blocking work, or unblock a task list.

## Workflow

1. List the tasks or workflow steps and their current status.
2. For each stuck item, identify what is blocking it: dependency, missing info, resource, decision.
3. Categorize blockers by type (people, process, tooling, information).
4. Determine who can unblock each one.
5. Prioritize blockers by impact on the overall goal.

## Output format

A blocker report: stuck items, blocker types, unblock owners, priority.

## Constraints

- Only report blockers grounded in the provided status.
- Be specific - vague blockers cannot be actioned.
