# Simplify

Reduce reading difficulty by simplifying vocabulary and sentence structure while preserving all key facts and nuance.

## When to use

- Rewrite dense content in simpler, more accessible language - replace jargon, shorten long sentences, prefer active voice - without losing substance or technical accuracy. Use when asked to simplify text, make it easier to read, or lower the reading level.

## Workflow

1. Read the text and mark jargon, long sentences, passive constructions, and complex structures.
2. Replace hard words with plain equivalents; define terms that must stay.
3. Split long sentences into shorter ones (aim for under ~20 words).
4. Prefer active voice and concrete nouns over abstractions.
5. Keep all key facts, numbers, and nuance intact.
6. Check readability: would a general reader follow without re-reading?

## Output format

Simplified text with the same meaning and facts, easier to read.

## Constraints

- Simpler language, never simpler thinking - preserve all substance and technical accuracy.
- Do not distort specialized content or drop important nuance and numbers.
