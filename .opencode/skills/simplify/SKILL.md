# Simplify

Reduce complexity of dense writing so more people can understand it, without losing substance.

## When to use

- Rewrite dense content in simpler, more accessible language. Use when asked to simplify text, make it easier to read, or lower the reading level of content.

## Workflow

1. Read the text and mark jargon, long sentences, and complex constructions.
2. Replace jargon with plain equivalents; define terms that must stay.
3. Split long sentences into shorter ones (aim for under ~20 words).
4. Use active voice and concrete nouns over abstractions.
5. Keep all key facts, numbers, and nuance intact.
6. Check readability: would a general reader follow without re-reading?

## Output format

Simplified text with the same meaning and facts.

## Constraints

- Simpler language, never simpler thinking - preserve all substance.
- Do not dumb down technical content to the point of inaccuracy.
