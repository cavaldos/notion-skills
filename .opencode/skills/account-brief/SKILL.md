# Account Brief

Compile a one-page account snapshot: who they are, what they need, where things stand.

## When to use

- Pull together a clean, one-page snapshot of an account so you walk into any conversation prepared. Use when asked to prepare an account brief, client snapshot, or pre-meeting account summary.

## Workflow

1. Gather account facts: company, industry, size, contacts.
2. Summarize the relationship: history, current work, health signals.
3. List current needs, pain points, and opportunities.
4. Note open action items and their owners.
5. Compress into a scannable one-page brief.

## Output format

A one-page account brief: overview, relationship, needs, opportunities, actions.

## Constraints

- Keep it to one page - executives read briefs, not reports.
- Only include verified account information.
