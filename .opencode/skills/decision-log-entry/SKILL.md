# Decision Log Entry

Record a decision in a clear, reusable format with context and ownership.

## When to use

- Capture a decision cleanly, with its rationale and owner. Use when asked to log a decision, write a decision record, or document a choice (ADR style).

## Workflow

1. State the decision in one unambiguous sentence.
2. Record the context: what problem or situation led to it.
3. List the options considered and why the chosen one won.
4. Note the consequences (positive and negative) and any follow-ups.
5. Assign the decision owner and date.
6. Format as a compact log entry (ADR-style).

## Output format

A structured decision log entry: Decision, Context, Options, Rationale, Consequences, Owner, Date.

## Constraints

- Stay factual - do not sugarcoat rejected options.
- Keep it short; a decision log is a record, not an essay.
