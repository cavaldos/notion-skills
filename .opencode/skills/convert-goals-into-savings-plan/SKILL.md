# Convert Goals into Savings Plan

Turn financial goals into a concrete, achievable savings plan.

## When to use

- Convert goals into a savings plan. Use when asked to create a savings plan, plan savings for a goal, or convert financial goals into a savings schedule.

## Workflow

1. Clarify each goal: what it is, target amount, target date.
2. Calculate the amount needed per month for each goal.
3. Sum required monthly savings and compare with income/expenses.
4. Prioritize goals if the total is not feasible.
5. Present a plan: goal, target, timeline, monthly contribution.

## Output format

A savings plan: goals with targets, timelines, and monthly contributions.

## Constraints

- Use only provided financial figures - no invented income.
- Flag infeasible goals honestly.
