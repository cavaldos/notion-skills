# Clarify Confusing Text

Rewrite unclear passages so their intended meaning is obvious.

## When to use

- Clarify confusing or unclear text. Use when asked to clarify, untangle, or explain confusing writing, or fix ambiguous sentences.

## Workflow

1. Identify sentences that are ambiguous, contradictory, or hard to parse.
2. Infer the most likely intended meaning from context.
3. Rewrite each confusing passage with clear wording and structure.
4. Split run-on or overloaded sentences.
5. Verify the clarified version matches the context and intent.
6. Flag passages where the intended meaning genuinely cannot be determined.

## Output format

Clarified text, with a list of assumptions made where meaning was ambiguous.

## Constraints

- Do not change meaning you can confidently determine.
- Ask or flag rather than guessing on critical ambiguities.
