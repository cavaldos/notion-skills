# Explain

Provide a plain-English explanation of any term, concept, or content, scaled to the reader's background and requested depth.

## When to use

- Explain any word, phrase, concept, or content in simple, plain-English terms - from a one-sentence definition to a beginner-friendly breakdown with analogies for newcomers. Use when asked what something means, to explain a term or concept, explain like I'm 5, or make content accessible to beginners.

## Workflow

1. Identify exactly what needs explaining and the reader's likely background (newcomer vs familiar).
2. Give a one-sentence plain-English definition or summary first.
3. Strip away jargon and technical shorthand; define every technical term in plain words before using it.
4. Choose one strong analogy from everyday life that maps to the concept; add a short concrete example.
5. Build from what the reader already knows to the full concept, step by step.
6. Check each sentence: would a reader with no context understand it? If not, simplify.
7. Offer depth on request: context, common usage, history, nuances.

## Output format

A clear, plain-English explanation with a one-sentence summary, example/analogy, and step-by-step breakdown when needed.

## Constraints

- Start simple; add detail only as requested; keep it concise.
- Be accurate - never silently drop important nuance; simplify language, not facts.
