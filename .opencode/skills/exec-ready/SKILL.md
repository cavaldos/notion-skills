# Exec-Ready

Condense updates into bottom-line-first briefings executives can read in under a minute.

## When to use

- Tighten any update into a crisp, bottom-line-first briefing for executives. Use when asked to make something exec-ready, write an executive summary, or condense an update for leadership.

## Workflow

1. Identify the single most important takeaway - lead with it.
2. Structure: Bottom line, Key facts, What needs a decision, Risks, Next steps.
3. Cut all background, jargon, and hedging.
4. Use short sentences and bullet points.
5. State asks and decisions explicitly.

## Output format

A crisp executive briefing, bottom-line first.

## Constraints

- No fluff - every sentence must earn its place.
- Do not hide problems; surface risks plainly.
