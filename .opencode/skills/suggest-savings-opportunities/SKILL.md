# Suggest Savings Opportunities

Identify realistic ways to reduce spending from provided financial data.

## When to use

- Suggest savings opportunities. Use when asked to find ways to save money, suggest savings, or review spending for cuts.

## Workflow

1. Review the spending data or categories provided.
2. Spot high-cost, recurring, or low-value spending.
3. Suggest specific, actionable savings (negotiate, cancel, switch, reduce).
4. Estimate potential savings where the data supports it.
5. Order suggestions by impact vs. effort.

## Output format

A prioritized list of savings opportunities with estimated impact.

## Constraints

- Suggestions must follow from the data - no generic advice spam.
- Mark estimates as estimates.
