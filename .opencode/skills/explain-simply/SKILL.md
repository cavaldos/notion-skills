# Explain Simply

Take any content or concept and explain it in simple, easy-to-understand terms.

## When to use

- Explain content in simple terms. Use when asked to explain something simply, make a concept easy to understand, or give a plain-English explanation.

## Workflow

1. Identify the concept and the reader's likely background.
2. Strip away jargon and technical shorthand.
3. Use everyday words, short sentences, and concrete examples.
4. Build from what the reader already knows to the new idea.
5. Keep the essential accuracy while simplifying presentation.

## Output format

A simple, clear explanation of the content or concept.

## Constraints

- Do not over-simplify to the point of inaccuracy.
- Keep it concise - simple does not mean rambling.
