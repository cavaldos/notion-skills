# TOEIC Writing — 63 phần Introduction cho tất cả đề trong "Toeic Essay Database"

> Nguồn đề: database **"Toeic Essay Database"** (trang *Write an opinion essay (8)*) — 63 đề.
> Tất cả introduction viết theo **form đã hướng dẫn** trong trang *"Hướng dẫn viết Mở đầu & Triển khai ý theo từng dạng đề TOEIC Writing"*, càng đồng nhất càng tốt.

---

## Các form được dùng (9 form chuẩn)

| Form | Dạng đề | Công thức |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **F1** | Agree/Disagree (đồng ý) | *"It is often argued that [X]. I completely agree with this statement, mainly because of two reasons."* |
| **F1b** | Agree/Disagree (phản đối) | *"While some people believe that [X], I personally disagree with this view for the following reasons."* |
| **F2** | Preference (chọn 1 trong 2) | *"When it comes to [chủ đề], people are divided between [A] and [B]. Personally, I believe [chọn] is the better option, mainly because..."* |
| **F3** | Advantages / Disadvantages | *"While [X] offers several benefits, it also has certain drawbacks that should be considered."* |
| **F4a** | Why / What (nguyên nhân) | *"There are several reasons why [hiện tượng]..."* |
| **F4b** | Why / What (yếu tố / cách làm) | *"When it comes to [chủ đề], several key factors/measures should be considered..."* |
| **F5** | 3-choice | *"While [X], [Y], and [Z] all play a role in [chủ đề], I believe [chọn] is the most crucial factor."* |
| **F6** | Ngân sách chính phủ (2 lựa chọn) | *"If I were to decide how to allocate the budget, I would choose to invest in [A] rather than [B]."* |
| **F7** | Discuss both views + opinion | *"While some people think [A], others believe [B]. Although both perspectives have merit, I personally side with [A/B]..."* |

## 1. Question 27 — E-books vs Printed books
**Dạng:** Preference (F2) — *phe chọn:* sách in (printed books)
**Đề:** "Which do you prefer: e-books or printed books? Give reasons or examples to support your opinion."

> When it comes to reading habits, people are divided between electronic books and printed books. Personally, I believe printed books are the better option, mainly because they are easier on the eyes and create a deeper, more focused reading experience.

**Ghi chú:** F2 · Body 1 = trải nghiệm đọc sâu & tốt cho mắt · Body 2 = cảm giác sở hữu / không phụ thuộc pin.
