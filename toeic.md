# TOEIC Writing — Opinion Essay

## Prompt

> If people want to be successful in their field, do they have to like what they do? Some say it is a must to enjoy working to succeed. But others claim that all they need is just hard work, whether they like it or not. Compare these two views and choose the one you prefer. Use specific reasons and examples to support your opinion.

## Essay

**Passion and Hard Work: The Keys to Success**

People have different opinions about what it takes to succeed in a career. Some believe that loving what you do is absolutely necessary, while others insist that hard work alone is enough, even if you do not enjoy your job. After comparing both views, I believe that although hard work is essential, a genuine love for your work is what makes lasting success possible.

Those who support the first view argue that passion gives people the energy and motivation to overcome difficulties. When we truly enjoy our work, we are willing to invest extra hours, learn new skills, and keep going after failures. For example, famous athletes like Michael Jordan practiced for hours every day not only because they wanted to win, but also because they genuinely loved playing basketball. Their passion turned hard work into pleasure, so they never gave up.

On the other hand, proponents of the second view claim that discipline and consistent effort matter more than feelings, which can change over time. Many successful professionals began their careers in jobs they did not like, yet they worked diligently and achieved great results. For instance, many business executives work long hours in demanding industries not because they enjoy every task, but because they are committed to their goals. Hard work itself can also create satisfaction, as competence and visible achievements bring a sense of pride.

While both arguments have merit, I prefer the first view because enjoyment is the fuel that keeps hard work sustainable in the long run. Working hard purely by willpower often leads to burnout, whereas passion naturally renews our energy. Moreover, people who love their jobs are more curious and creative, which helps them adapt to a rapidly changing world. Steve Jobs is a good example: his deep love for design and technology drove him to create revolutionary products and to persist even after he was fired from his own company.

In conclusion, hard work is certainly a necessary ingredient of success, but it is most powerful when combined with a genuine passion for what we do. Since enjoying our work gives us the inner drive to work hard year after year, I am convinced that liking what we do is the stronger key to achievement.

*(~330 words)*

# Cấu trúc mẫu (Template Structures)

## 📌 1. Mở bài (Introduction)

| # | Cấu trúc mẫu | Ý nghĩa | Cách thay thế |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **People have different opinions about** what it takes to succeed in a career. | Mọi người có nhiều ý kiến khác nhau về... | *There is a long-running debate about...* / *Views differ on...* |
| 2 | **Some believe that** loving what you do is necessary, **while others insist that** hard work is enough. | Một số tin... trong khi những người khác khẳng định... | *Some argue that... whereas others claim that...* / *While some say..., others maintain that...* |

| 17 | **Since enjoying our work gives us the inner drive..., I am convinced that...** | Vì..., tôi tin chắc rằng... (kết luận khép lại ý kiến) | *As..., I firmly believe that...* / *For this reason, I am persuaded that...* |

## ⭐ Bộ 5 "cấu trúc vàng" nên thuộc lòng

1. **Some believe that... , while others insist that...** → mở bài nêu 2 quan điểm
2. **Those who support the first view argue that...** → đoạn quan điểm 1
3. **On the other hand, proponents of the second view claim that...** → đoạn quan điểm 2
4. **While both arguments have merit, I prefer... because...** → đoạn ý kiến cá nhân
5. **In conclusion, ... is certainly necessary, but...** → kết bài

> Với 5 cấu trúc này, chỉ cần thay phần nội dung là có thể viết được bất kỳ đề opinion essay nào của TOEIC (môi trường, công nghệ, giáo dục, công việc...).
